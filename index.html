From Integration to Insights: AI-Ready Data Integration with Informatica and Snowflake

As organizations adopt the Snowflake AI Data Cloud to power advanced analytics, GenAI, and Agentic AI workloads, building scalable, trusted, and cost-efficient data pipelines becomes critical. Legacy ETL tools and fragmented point solutions derail AI initiatives and increase cost and complexity.

  • Build AI-ready data pipelines using Informatica by efficiently ingesting data into Snowflake
  • Leverage native pushdown processing to Snowflake for high-performance transformations at scale
  • Integrate batch and streaming data across hybrid and multi-cloud environments
  • Orchestrate complex data pipelines to support analytics, GenAI, and Agentic AI use cases
  • Apply metadata-driven governance, data quality, and observability to deliver trusted, AI ready data
